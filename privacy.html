Privacy Policy

Paint.io · Effective 28 September 2026

The short version
  • We don't have accounts or servers, and we don't collect your personal information.
  • Your progress is saved on your iPhone and in your own iCloud account.
  • Ads come from Google AdMob. They're always non-personalised and kid-safe, and the game never tracks you.
  • The one-time "Remove Ads" purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • You can delete your progress at any time from the game's Settings.

Who we are

Paint.io is made by Gary Lam, an independent developer ("we", "us"). You can reach us at paintio1034@gmail.com.

Information the game keeps

To remember your progress, the game stores your coins, equipment, settings, missions, play statistics (such as rounds played and territory painted) and the days you've watched an optional ad towards an unlock. This is stored:

  • On your iPhone, inside the app.
  • In your iCloud account, if you're signed in to iCloud, so your progress follows you to other devices. This is kept by Apple in your own iCloud storage; we can't see or access it.

We don't run servers, and none of this is sent to us.

Game Center

Signing in to Apple's Game Center is optional. If you do, your scores are sent to Game Center's leaderboards, where other players can see them along with your Game Center nickname. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. You can manage it in your iPhone's Settings, under Game Center.

Advertising

The game shows ads from Google AdMob to keep it free: a banner during play, and optional video ads that you choose to watch, for example to unlock the Bamboo Brushmaster avatar. We set every ad request as directed to children and as coming from a user under the age of consent. This means:

  • ads are non-personalised (not based on your interests or behaviour) and limited to content suitable for all ages;
  • the game never asks to track you and doesn't use your device's advertising identifier.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may receive limited technical information from your device, such as your IP address (which can indicate your approximate location), device type, operating system, and how ads are viewed or tapped. Where the law requires it, Google may show a consent form before any ads load. Google handles this under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

In-app purchase

You can buy "Remove Ads" once to turn off the banner. The purchase is made and processed by Apple through the App Store, under Apple's Privacy Policy. We never receive your name, payment details or Apple Account. Apple only tells the game whether the purchase has been made, so it can hide the ads, and you can restore it on another device from the game's "Remove ads" screen.

Crash reports

If you've chosen in your iPhone's settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. These don't identify you. We use them only to fix problems.

Children

Paint.io is suitable for players of all ages, including children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included. Ads are configured for children as described above. If you're a parent and have a question, contact us at the address above.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your personal information.
  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We don't use analytics services in the game.

Your choices

  • Delete your progress: in the game, open Settings and tap Delete my data. This erases it from your iPhone and your iCloud account. It doesn't affect a "Remove Ads" purchase, which stays with your Apple Account.
  • Game Center: sign out or manage your data in the Settings app, under Game Center.
  • iCloud: you can stop the game using iCloud in the Settings app, under your name › iCloud.

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, UK or California), you may have rights to access or delete information about you. Since we don't hold personal information, most requests are handled by the steps above, by Apple or by Google, but you're always welcome to contact us.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it on this page and change the date at the top.
